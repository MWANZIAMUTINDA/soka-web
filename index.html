SOKA FC League Manager

Run your FC league

A tournament app for friends who play EA SPORTS FC: match days, availability, fair fixtures, confirmed results, live tables, rivalries and XP.

01

Match Days

Set the date and see who is in. Everyone answers once, so you know who is playing before you start.

02

Live Tables

Standings update as soon as a result is confirmed by both players, or decided by an admin.

03

Rivalries & XP

Head-to-head records, badges and XP that build up with every confirmed match.

04

Fair Fixtures

Round robin, knockout or groups, drawn fairly from the players who are available.
