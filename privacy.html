Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. This policy explains what the SOKA app collects, why, who processes it, and how you can delete it.

Who we are

SOKA is an independent app made in Kenya. Contact: silvestermutinda74@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile: username, optional display name and optional profile photo.
  • League activity: tournaments you create or join, your role, match-day availability, fixtures, scores you enter or confirm, disputes and admin decisions, standings, statistics, XP and badges.
  • Match-day bill and friendly stakes (optional): games you played, your share of the bill, stakes you choose to join (18+ only) and how they settled. SOKA does not take or hold payments; any money is settled between players outside the app.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token for your device and the notification types you muted.
  • Technical: basic logs our hosting providers keep to run the service securely (for example IP address and request times).

We do not collect your location, contacts, or payment card details, and we do not show ads or sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and your leagues (the service you asked for).
  • To send the notifications you allow (match days, results to confirm, rivalries). You can mute each type in Settings or turn them off on your phone.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example limiting repeated invite-code guesses).

Who can see it

  • Members of a tournament see each other's username, display name, photo and that tournament's fixtures, results, standings, bills and stakes.
  • Tournament admins also see availability replies and disputes for their tournament.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in the EU, Ireland), Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering push notifications), and an email provider for account emails.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your sign-in, email, profile photo, push tokens and notifications. Match results you took part in stay in your friends' tables, shown as "Deleted player", so their standings remain correct. Security logs are kept for a limited period.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account) or via the account deletion page.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for corrections, by emailing silvestermutinda74@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya).

Children

SOKA is for people aged 13 and over. Friendly stakes are only available to users who confirm they are 18 or older.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is limited by database rules so players only see their own tournaments.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
