Delete your SOKA account

SOKA is a tournament app for friends who play EA SPORTS FC. You can delete your account at any time.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open SOKA and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out and your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address you used to sign up. We confirm the request with you and delete the account within 7 days.

Email a deletion request

Address: silvestermutinda74@gmail.com · Subject: “Delete my SOKA account”

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and email address (removed within 24 hours of the request in the app).
  • Your username, display name and profile photo.
  • Your device push tokens, notifications and notification settings.

What is kept, and why

  • Results of matches you played stay in your friends’ league tables and history, shown as “Deleted player” with no link to you. Removing them would change other players’ standings.
  • A tamper-proof audit record of result changes, without your name or email.

Backups that may still contain your data are overwritten within 7 days.
